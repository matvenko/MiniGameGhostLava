# Fox robot — temporary playable character

The authoritative references are `approved-reference.png`, `reference-front.png`, `reference-side-left.png` and `reference-side-right.png`. The model uses genuine volumetric geometry: a continuous coral/ivory housing with a front opening, convex glass screen and rounded bezel, thick recessed ears, integrated paw caps, and a curved segmented tail. The whole character faces diagonally upward. The earlier loose approximation has been replaced.

`FoxRobot.blend` is the editable Blender source. `build.py` calls `reference_geometry.py` and `bake_occlusion.py`, builds the armature and animations, then exports `FoxRobot.fbx`. Vertex ambient occlusion preserves the depth of recesses in the lightweight `FoxPorcelain.shader`. Blender previews are `portrait_final.png`, `side_final.png` and `overhead_final.png`. Unity previews are `unity_portrait.png`, `unity_detail.png`, `unity_overhead.png` and `unity_showoff.png`. The Unity capture uses an sRGB output target so PNGs are not incorrectly darkened.

`ImportInstall.cs.txt` installs the FBX, materials, controller and prefab at `Assets/Characters/FoxRobot/`, activates the fox under the existing Ghost in LavaScene, and assigns it to the main-menu portrait. The previous LanternWarden remains inactive. LoadingScreenController retains the fox shader in its portrait. The model child is centered with local Z +0.19 at uniform visual scale 0.69 (about 6% larger).

The 33 meshes contain 84,154 triangles and 12 bones. The five clips are idle, move, caught, death and Fox_ShowOff. Locomotion has a buoyant two-step bob, alternating paw strokes, body sway, ear follow-through and a delayed three-bone tail wag. Secondary steering is limited to 2 degrees forward lean and 1.5 degrees bank so the larger silhouette stays in its cell. Show-off perks the ears, wags the tail and changes the face before returning to idle; its controller state is `show_off`. A shop character-selection preview does not yet exist, so tap-to-preview integration is still future work.

Validation: 31 sampled poses per idle and move clip stay within a 0.497 m planar radius about the player root, including the maximum secondary lean and bank, fitting a centered 1 m tile at every heading. Animation loop endpoints match. Play Mode confirmed the active fox, active animator, 33 renderers, persistent centering and the show_off state, with no console errors. This was a smoke test, not a complete gameplay or device-performance regression. The previews are the actual current model, not the reference image or a billboard.
